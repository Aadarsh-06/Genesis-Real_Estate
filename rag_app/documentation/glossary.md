# Home Loan & Tax Glossary

This glossary defines key terms related to home loans and tax benefits in India.

---

## HRA (House Rent Allowance)

**Definition**: A component of a salary package provided by an employer to meet the cost of a rented house used by the employee for their residence.

**Taxability**: It is partially or fully exempt under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act, based on specific calculations:
- Actual HRA received
- 40-50% of salary (50% for metro cities, 40% for non-metro)
- Rent paid minus 10% of salary

**Important**: HRA can only be claimed if the employee is actually staying in a rented accommodation.

---

## Standard Deduction (on Rental Income)

**Definition**: A flat deduction of 30% allowed on the "Net Annual Value" of a let-out property.

**Purpose**: This is intended to cover repairs and maintenance, regardless of the actual amount spent by the owner.

**Constraint**: This is separate from the interest deduction under Section 24(b).

---

## NAV (Net Annual Value)

**Definition**: The amount for which a property might reasonably be expected to let from year to year, minus any municipal taxes paid by the owner.

**Calculation**: 
```
Gross Annual Value (Actual Rent or Fair Rent) - Municipal Taxes = NAV
```

**Important**: For a self-occupied property, the NAV is always considered **Zero**.

---

## Pre-Construction Period

**Definition**: The period starting from the date of borrowing the home loan and ending on the 31st of March immediately preceding the date of completion of construction (or acquisition).

**Importance**: This defines the "bucket" of interest that must be spread over 5 years for tax claims.

**Example**: If you took a loan in April 2022 and got possession in June 2024:
- Pre-construction period: April 2022 to March 2024
- Interest paid during this period is claimed in 5 equal installments starting FY 2024-25

---

## Self-Occupied Property (SOP)

**Definition**: A property used by the owner for their own residence throughout the year.

**Tax Impact**: 
- As of current laws, an individual can declare up to **two houses** as "Self-Occupied"
- NAV of self-occupied property is considered Zero
- Any additional houses beyond two are treated as "Deemed Let Out"

---

## Deemed Let Out Property (DLOP)

**Definition**: If a taxpayer owns more than two residential properties, the additional properties are "deemed" to be rented out, even if they are vacant.

**Tax Impact**: 
- The owner must pay tax on the "Fair Rent" (market value) that the property could have earned
- Tax is applicable even if no rent was actually received

**Example**: If you own 3 houses and self-occupy 2, the 3rd house is automatically treated as let-out for tax purposes.

---

## Stamp Duty & Registration Charges

**Definition**: Mandatory legal fees paid to the state government to transfer property title and record the transaction in official records.

**Tax Link**: 
- Deductible under Section 80C in the year they are paid
- Can be claimed even if the home loan is not yet active
- Subject to the overall ₹1.5 Lakh limit of Section 80C

---

## Co-Borrower vs. Co-Owner

### Co-Owner
A person who has a **legal share in the title** of the property.

### Co-Borrower
A person who is **responsible for the repayment** of the loan.

### Tax Rule
To claim tax benefits, an individual must be **BOTH**:
1. A co-owner (ownership share in property)
2. A co-borrower (liability for loan repayment)

**Important**: Being just a co-borrower (without ownership) does **NOT** entitle one to tax deductions.

---

## Possession vs. Sanction

### Sanction Date
**Definition**: The date the bank approves the loan.

**Importance**: Crucial for determining eligibility under:
- Section 80EE (loans sanctioned in FY 2016-17)
- Section 80EEA (loans sanctioned between April 2019 - March 2022)

### Possession Date
**Definition**: The date the builder hands over the keys to the buyer.

**Importance**: Crucial for:
- Starting Section 24(b) interest claims
- Starting Section 80C principal claims
- Calculating pre-construction interest period

**Timeline Rule**: The 5-year completion rule under Section 24(b) is measured from the end of the financial year in which the loan was taken.

## How Genesis Uses These Terms

The Genesis model simplifies tax calculations by assuming:
- Self-occupied property only
- Full Section 24(b) and 80C benefits available
- 30% tax bracket for maximum benefit calculation
- No pre-EMI/pre-construction interest (lump sum payment assumed)

For complex scenarios involving co-borrowers, multiple properties, or pre-construction interest, consult a tax professional.
